  ## 💬 How I Communicate

  ### Slack
  - I prefer async communication, but work however suits you best
  - Tag me directly if you need input - I won't see everything
  - 👀 means I've seen it and will reply later
  - I sometimes send messages outside business hours (family schedule or urgent issues), but there's zero expectation to respond until your next working
  day - unless it's a production incident
  - I share regular updates with leadership and stakeholders - the more you keep me in the loop proactively, the less I need to chase you for context

  **What I need back:**
  - Acknowledge messages when tagged - anything counts: 👀, 👍 ,a one-word reply. No reaction at all is the one thing that doesn't work for me.
  - If we've agreed you'll come back with an answer and it's not coming - just say so. "Busy", "later", "wrong person" are all fine. Silence isn't.

  This isn't about speed - it's about signal. Not getting any reaction on something I've written or answered makes me anxious, and I'd rather hear "can't
  right now" than hear nothing.

  ### 1:1s
  - Weekly, 30-45 minutes (but if anything urgent please don't wait, let's talk asap)
  - Your agenda > mine
  - We will have a shared document for notes and action items
  - **Focused on:** how you're feeling, what you're learning, what's blocking you
  - **Less on:** status updates (I can read those)

  ### Meetings
  - If I call one, expect context and goals shared upfront
  - If I seem distracted, call it out - it usually means I'm context-switching too much and need to fix my calendar

  ### Feedback
  - I use the SBIE framework - Situation, Behavior, Impact, Expectation

  - Direct but kind, usually in 1:1s unless time-sensitive

  **What I'm working on:** I sometimes soften feedback too much - please ask me to be clearer if needed

  ## 🧠 How I Make Decisions

  **The key question:** [_Can we reverse this?_](https://shit.management/one-way-and-two-way-door-decisions/)

  - **Two-way doors** (reversible): move fast, test, learn, iterate
  - **One-way doors** (hard to reverse): slow down, gather input, think harder

  > "Move fast" never means skipping quality - it means streamlining safety nets.

  **Decision ownership**
  - Default: The person closest to the problem. You have the context, you own the outcome, you decide.
  - I step in to stress-test, not to take over
  - I'll override only for safety, compliance, or misalignment with company strategy
  - I ask _"why"_ a lot - it's curiosity, not criticism

  ## 🤝 What I Need From You

  **Non-negotiables**
  - When you ship something, tell me what moved - not what merged. "Conversion improved by X" is more useful to me than "feature deployed".
  - If your estimate is slipping, tell me on Monday, not Friday. I can help on Monday. By Friday I can only escalate.
  - Disagree openly, but assume good intent. I'd rather have a tense conversation on Tuesday than discover resentment in a retro.

  **Preferences (not mandates)**
  - Write things down - helps me process
  - Ask questions publicly - helps others learn
  - Default to sharing context I didn't ask for. I'd rather filter noise than miss signal.
  - Share work-in-progress - I love seeing process, but it's optional

  If my defaults don't work for you, tell me what does.

  ## 🎁 What You Can Expect From Me

  - **No performance review surprises.** If something's off, you'll hear it in our next 1:1, not months later in a formal review.
  - **I'll fight for your growth, compensation, and recognition** - even when it's uncomfortable for me. That's the job.
  - **Context, not just direction.** I'll share why we're doing something, what the constraints are, and what I know about what's coming - including the
  difficult parts.
  - **I'll absorb organisational noise** so you can focus. But I can't shield you from everything - sometimes the noise is the work.

  What I *can't* guarantee: perfect clarity on every project, or that every task will feel deeply meaningful. I'm working within constraints too. If goals
   feel fuzzy, that's on me - push me on it.

  ## 🪞 How to Give Me Feedback

  I genuinely want it - here's what helps:

  - **Specific > vague** - "Yesterday in stand-up..." beats "You don't listen"
  - **Timely > batched** - don't wait for 1:1s
  - **Private > public** - but I can handle public if needed
  - **Impact-framed** - "When you do X, I feel Y" helps me adjust
  - If I've gone quiet on *you*, call it out. I hold myself to the same standard.

  ## ❤️ When I'm Not at My Best

  **Stress signals**
  - I go quiet in meetings (I'm overthinking)
  - I ask the same question twice (I'm not processing well)
  - I jump to solutions before understanding (I'm anxious about delivery)

  **What helps**
  - Ask directly: "You seem tense - what's going on?"
  - Clarify: "Do you want me to listen or problem-solve?"
  - Push back kindly: "That's not the right priority right now."
